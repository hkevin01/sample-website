Our story

A local coffee bar built around good conversation.

Range Finder Coffee began with a simple idea: bring a thoughtful, welcoming coffee experience to the heart of the neighborhood. We roast for balance, brew for clarity, and keep the room warm, easy, and full of life.

Whether you are grabbing a quick espresso before work or settling in with a pastry and a book, we aim to make every visit feel easy.

Single-origin beans

Rotating roasts chosen for sweetness, acidity, and character.

Local pastries

Fresh daily favorites from nearby bakers and kitchen partners.

Community rhythm

Live sets, reading nights, and neighborhood gatherings all season long.

Warm atmosphere

Thoughtful details, comfortable seating, and a room that invites you in.
