Our story

A local coffee bar built around good conversation.

Range Finder Coffee began with a simple idea: bring a thoughtful, welcoming coffee experience to the heart of the neighborhood. We roast for balance, brew for clarity, and keep the room warm, easy, and full of life.

Whether you are grabbing a quick espresso before work or settling in with a pastry and a book, we aim to make every visit feel easy.

Single-origin beans

Rotating roasts chosen for sweetness, acidity, and character.

Local pastries

Fresh daily favorites from nearby bakers and kitchen partners.

Community rhythm

Live sets, reading nights, and neighborhood gatherings all season long.

Warm atmosphere

Thoughtful details, comfortable seating, and a room that invites you in.

Operations model

Built for a smooth neighborhood café experience.

We combined a fast static front end with the operational habits people actually need: reliable updates, accessibility, secure content workflows, and easy maintenance.

Zero-framework static site

Fast front-end delivery with no heavy build requirements and very little runtime complexity.

Browser-based content tools

Simple admin patterns let staff update menu, events, and announcements without technical overhead.

WCAG-ready accessibility

Keyboard-friendly navigation, high-contrast controls, and semantic structure for easier access.

Multi-layer security

Secure defaults, rate-limiting ideas, and strong response headers support safer deployment patterns.

Soft-delete recovery

Content can be rolled back if a change is mistaken, reducing the risk of accidental loss.

Backup-first workflow

Snapshots before meaningful changes provide a practical restore mechanism for operational continuity.

Full audit trail

Every staff action can be tracked, which supports accountability and post-change review.

Event calendar support

Seasonal programming, workshops, and community events stay visible without constant manual updates.

Image upload safeguards

Standard image checks help protect the site from malformed files and predictable assets.
